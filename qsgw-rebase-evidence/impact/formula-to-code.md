## Fixed-basis adapter invariants

The candidate must separately prove these QSGW-only invariants; no shared-core edit may be used to enforce them:

1. `mf0`, `C0`, and `v0` are immutable after iteration zero.
2. Upstream G/W/Sigma/EXX/Hartree builders consume the live mean field each iteration.
3. Sigma, EXX, Vc and delta-VH are represented in or projected to the fixed `mf0` basis before Hamiltonian assembly.
4. Diagonalization returns unitary `U`; live WFC and velocity are updated as `C=C0 U` and `v=U^dagger v0 U`.
5. Hermiticity, orthogonality, basis/gauge binding and projection round-trip residuals are recorded per iteration.
